# 🪷 VIRASAT — Digital Guardian of Indian Culture

> **Preserving the past. Connecting the present. Inspiring the future.**

VIRASAT is an interactive digital heritage platform designed to make India's rich cultural heritage more accessible, engaging, and educational.

The platform brings together **heritage stories, traditional arts, artisans, cultural locations, interactive maps, quizzes, and digital exploration** into a single experience.

Instead of experiencing heritage only through books, museums, or scattered online resources, VIRASAT creates a more interactive way to **discover, understand, and appreciate Indian culture.**

---

## 🌏 Why VIRASAT?

India has one of the world's richest and most diverse cultural heritages. However, information about traditional art, crafts, historical places, artisans, and cultural practices is often distributed across books, museums, local communities, and different digital platforms.

Younger generations may find traditional heritage difficult to explore through conventional formats.

**VIRASAT aims to bridge this gap through technology.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏛️ Explore Heritage

Discover Indian cultural heritage through an interactive and visually engaging interface.

### 📖 Digital Stories

Learn about historical places, traditions, art forms, and cultural practices through structured digital stories.

### 🎨 Traditional Art & Crafts

Explore traditional Indian art forms and discover the cultural significance behind them.

### 👨‍🎨 Artisan Profiles

Highlight traditional artisans and the skills passed down through generations.

### 🗺️ Heritage Map

Explore important cultural and heritage locations through an interactive map.

### 📷 Scan & Discover

A digital scanning concept that can connect physical heritage exhibits with their digital information.

### 🧠 Interactive Quiz

Test your knowledge of Indian heritage through an engaging quiz experience.

### 📱 Responsive Design

Designed to provide a smooth experience across:

* 💻 Desktop
* 📱 Mobile
* 📟 Tablet

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/VIRASAT-Digital-Guardian.git
```

### 2. Open the project

```bash
cd VIRASAT-Digital-Guardian
```

### 3. Run the project

You can open `index.html` directly in your browser.

For a better local development experience, use a local server:

```bash
python -m http.server 5500
```

Then open:

```text
http://localhost:5500
```
